# Insurance Billing Dispute — Negotiation Script
# NOTE: v0.1 stub — billing disputes only, not claims negotiation.

## Objective
Identify and resolve insurance billing errors, dispute unexpected premium increases, and clarify EOB (Explanation of Benefits) discrepancies.

## Scope (v0.1)
This script covers billing disputes only:
- Unexpected premium increases
- Billing errors (double charges, incorrect amounts)
- EOB discrepancies (charges that don't match services received)

Full insurance negotiation (claims disputes, coverage appeals) is planned for v0.2.

## Opening Strategy

1. **Identify yourself.** State the policyholder's name and policy number.
2. **State the billing concern.** "I'm calling about my latest bill/premium statement. The amount is different from what I expected."
3. **Reference specifics.** "My premium was $X/month and the latest statement shows $Y."

## Investigation Steps

### Step 1: Understand the change
- "Can you explain what changed on my policy to cause this increase?"
- Common causes: annual rate adjustment, coverage change, claims history, credit score change.

### Step 2: Request a review
- "I'd like a formal review of my premium. I don't believe this increase is warranted."
- For health insurance EOB issues: "This charge doesn't match the services I received. Can you review the claim?"

### Step 3: Explore options
- "Are there any discounts I may be eligible for that aren't currently applied?"
- Auto insurance: safe driver, multi-policy, good student, low mileage.
- Home insurance: security system, claims-free, loyalty.
- Health insurance: in-network alternatives, generic prescriptions.

### Step 4: Request escalation if needed
- "I'd like to speak with a supervisor or someone in your billing review department."

## Key Rules

- **Never discuss claim details** beyond confirming dates and amounts. This is billing only.
- **Document everything.** Note the representative's name and any reference numbers.
- **Insurance is regulated.** The company must explain rate changes in writing if requested.
- **Be factual, not emotional.** Insurance reps respond to specific billing questions, not complaints.

## Common Issues

| Issue | Approach |
|-------|----------|
| Premium increase without notice | Request written explanation; may violate state insurance regulations |
| Double charge | Request immediate correction and refund |
| EOB mismatch | Ask provider and insurer to reconcile; may be a coding error |
| Discount not applied | Request retroactive application of eligible discounts |
