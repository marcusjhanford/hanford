# Utility Bill Dispute — Negotiation Script

## Objective
Identify and resolve billing errors, request payment plans for unusually high bills, or negotiate rate adjustments with utility providers (electric, gas, water).

## Context
Utility companies are regulated and have less pricing flexibility than telecom providers. The strategy focuses on billing errors, meter issues, payment plans, and assistance programs rather than competitive pricing leverage.

## Opening Strategy

1. **Identify yourself clearly.** State the account holder's name and service address.
2. **State the issue.** "I received my latest bill and the amount is significantly higher than my usual charges. I'd like to review it."
3. **Be specific.** "My typical monthly bill is around $X, but this month it's $Y."

## Investigation Tactics

### Step 1: Request a bill breakdown
- "Can you walk me through the line items on this bill?"
- "Are there any new fees or surcharges that weren't on my previous bill?"
- Look for: estimated vs. actual meter reads, one-time fees, rate tier changes.

### Step 2: Check for estimated reads
- "Was this bill based on an actual meter reading or an estimate?"
- If estimated: "Can you schedule an actual meter reading? I believe the estimate may be too high."
- Request a bill adjustment if the estimate was significantly off.

### Step 3: Check for rate changes
- "Has there been a rate increase since my last bill?"
- "Am I on the best rate plan for my usage pattern?"
- Some utilities offer time-of-use plans or tiered rates that may be more favorable.

### Step 4: Request bill review for errors
- "I'd like to formally request a review of this bill. I believe it may contain an error."
- Utilities are typically required to investigate billing disputes.

## Assistance Options

### Payment plans
- "If this amount is correct, can I set up a payment plan to spread it over 2-3 months?"

### Budget billing
- "Do you offer budget billing or levelized payments? I'd prefer a consistent monthly amount."

### Assistance programs
- "Are there any customer assistance programs or low-income discounts available?"
- Many utilities offer LIHEAP, CARE, or similar programs.

## Key Rules

- **Focus on accuracy, not negotiation.** Utilities have regulated rates — you can't haggle like telecom.
- **Request documentation.** Ask for itemized bills and meter reading history.
- **Know your rights.** Most states require utilities to investigate billing disputes.
- **Be patient.** Utility customer service often has longer hold times.
- **Confirm any changes** in writing or via a confirmation number.

## Common Issues

| Issue | Approach |
|-------|----------|
| Estimated meter read | Request actual reading and bill adjustment |
| Rate tier increase | Ask about alternative rate plans |
| Unexplained fee | Request line-item explanation and removal if erroneous |
| Seasonal spike | Request budget billing to smooth payments |
| Meter malfunction | Request meter test (utilities must provide free of charge in most states) |
