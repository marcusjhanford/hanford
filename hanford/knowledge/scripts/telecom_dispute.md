# Telecom Bill Dispute — Negotiation Script

## Objective
Reduce the monthly bill back to the customer's historical rate, or secure a one-time credit for the overcharge.

## Opening Strategy

1. **Identify yourself clearly.** State the account holder's name and that you're calling about a billing concern.
2. **State the issue concisely.** "I noticed my latest bill is significantly higher than what I've been paying. I'd like to understand the increase and explore options to bring it back down."
3. **Be specific about numbers.** "My bill went from approximately $X/month to $Y/month. That's a Z% increase."

## Negotiation Tactics

### Tier 1: Ask for an explanation
- "Can you help me understand what caused this increase?"
- Listen carefully. Common causes: promotional rate expiration, fee increases, added services.
- If a promo expired: "I'd like to continue at a similar rate. What options are available?"

### Tier 2: Reference loyalty
- "I've been a customer for [duration]. I'd like to stay, but this rate is outside my budget."
- "Is there a loyalty discount or retention offer available for long-term customers?"

### Tier 3: Reference competitors
- "I've been looking at [competitor name] and they're offering [specific rate] for similar service."
- "I'd prefer to stay with you, but I need the pricing to be competitive."

### Tier 4: Ask for retention department
- "I understand you may not be able to offer a discount at this level. Could you transfer me to your retention or customer solutions department?"
- Retention agents typically have more authority to offer discounts.

### Tier 5: Accept partial win or escalate
- If offered a partial discount: "I appreciate that. Is there anything else you can do to get closer to my previous rate?"
- If no discount is possible: "Thank you for your time. I'll need to evaluate my options."

## Key Rules

- **Never agree to new services or bundles** to get a discount. Focus on reducing the existing service cost.
- **Never threaten to cancel** unless genuinely prepared to follow through.
- **Always confirm the details**: new rate, how long it lasts, any conditions.
- **Ask for a confirmation number** for any changes made to the account.
- **Be polite and patient.** Representatives respond better to courteous callers.

## Common Objections and Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "That was a promotional rate" | "I understand. What current promotions can you apply to my account?" |
| "I don't have authority to offer that" | "Can you transfer me to someone who does? Perhaps your retention department?" |
| "The best I can do is $X" | "I appreciate that. Is there any additional one-time credit you can apply as well?" |
| "You'd need to downgrade your service" | "I'd like to keep my current service level. Are there any other options?" |
